Note on Matcha: Green Tea Powder, Not Coffee Beans

Matcha is made from finely stone-ground, shade-grown Japanese green tea leaves rich in chlorophyll and antioxidants. It contains clean natural tea caffeine and zero espresso, unless you order the "Dirty Matcha" (Matcha + Espresso shot).

Brewing Reference

Know Your Coffee

Understand exact ingredient ratios, milk texturing, caffeine profiles, and caloric estimates before you order.

Espresso

Concentrated extraction forced under 9 bars of pressure. Rich, thick crema and maximum flavor density.

Ratio (Espresso : Water) 1 : 0
Strength: 5/5 (Very Strong) · Caffeine: High
Milk: None · Sweetness: Zero · Calories: ~2 kcal
Best Time: Early morning / Quick afternoon boost

Cappuccino

Equal thirds of rich espresso, sweet steamed milk, and a thick cushion of velvety microfoam.

Ratio (Espresso : Milk : Foam) 1 : 1 : 1
Strength: 4/5 (Strong) · Caffeine: Medium-High
Milk: Equal parts steamed & froth · Calories: ~130 kcal
Best Time: Traditional breakfast beverage

Café Latte

Generous steamed milk over espresso with a thin microfoam sheet. Milkier, softer, and smoother than a cappuccino.

Ratio (Espresso : Milk : Foam) 1 : 3 : 1
Strength: 3/5 (Medium) · Caffeine: Medium
Milk: High volume steamed milk · Calories: ~165 kcal
Best Time: Mid-morning or relaxing afternoon

Americano

Double shot of espresso diluted with pure hot water. Retains the nuanced bean character with zero dairy.

Ratio (Espresso : Hot Water) 1 : 2
Strength: 4/5 (Strong) · Caffeine: High
Water: Filtered hot water · Calories: ~5 kcal
Best Time: All-day clean sipping

Flat White

Double ristretto combined seamlessly with velvety microfoam. Coffee-forward punch with a smooth texture.

Ratio (Double Ristretto : Microfoam) 1 : 2 : 0
Strength: 4/5 (Strong) · Caffeine: High
Milk: Microfoam only, no stiff froth · Calories: ~125 kcal
Best Time: Morning focus & work sprint

Matcha Latte (Green Tea)

100% shade-grown green tea powder from Uji, Japan whisked with steamed milk. Clean sustained calm alertness.

Composition (Matcha : Milk) No Coffee
Strength: 2/5 (Mild) · Caffeine: Tea Polyphenols
Base: 1st Harvest Green Tea Leaf · Calories: ~120 kcal
Best Time: Afternoon focus without coffee jitters

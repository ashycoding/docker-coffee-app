Origin & Heritage

The Indian Specialty Coffee Legacy

India is one of the few coffee-producing regions in the world where 100% of the coffee is cultivated under natural shade canopies. Sourced from high-elevation estates across the Western and Eastern Ghats—including Chikmagalur, Coorg, Araku Valley, and the Nilgiris—our coffees develop dense bean structures and balanced sweetness.

Single-Estate Traceability

Direct sourcing from verified estates and cooperatives with transparent lot identification and elevation data.

Micro-Batch Roasting

Roasted in small 5kg batches to calibrate airflow, drum velocity, and thermal drop temperatures precisely.

Containerized Architecture

Production-ready container deployment ensuring decoupled services, reproducible builds, and high availability.
